# Reviewing a schedule on desktop

An ordinary single-character schedule with **Show activity description** or **Show activity rules** enabled now has one full-width detail pane. Choose **Details** above a period to review its assigned activity. Changing a dropdown also switches the detail pane to that period. The pane keeps the complete authored description, including requirement clauses, and any enabled cost/effect rules; long text scrolls in one place.

Reviewing a period does not change the plan. Changing its activity edits the existing draft only. The game's progression button still determines when the plan is confirmed and whether an activity starts immediately. Completed periods can be reviewed, but their dropdowns remain disabled. Tab reaches the period controls and the detail pane; the native and Web player retain standard dropdown keyboard behavior.

The existing description/rules switches remain authoritative. Schedules with both off retain their original columns. Compact reading layouts and multi-character schedules keep their current planning flows. There is no data conversion or new save format. Rule summaries describe authored costs and effects; they do not predict future event results or evaluate an authored prose requirement as a live availability check.

Allow enough vertical space for selectors, the draft explanation and the detail pane. Review the longest description and full requirement text in the exported player, including narrow windows and the keyboard. For phones, author a readable layout and compact planning as described in [Phone reading layouts](phone-reading-layout.md).
